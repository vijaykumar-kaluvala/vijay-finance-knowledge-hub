About the Research Lab

Bringing artificial intelligence and financial knowledge together.

Our Vision

To develop an academic platform that encourages innovative thinking, practical financial experimentation and interdisciplinary research at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Finance.

Our Mission

To support students and researchers through financial AI applications, research projects, data-driven analysis, market simulation and the development of practical financial knowledge.
