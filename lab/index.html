HOW TO USE THIS STUDIO

Student quick guide

① Prepare

Arrange your data in rows. Put variable names in the first row and one observation in each subsequent row. Keep numeric values in numeric columns.

② Analyse

Upload the CSV. Review descriptive statistics, inspect correlations and select X and Y for regression.

③ Interpret

Explain results in the context of your research question. Check data quality, sample size and assumptions before drawing conclusions.

Method notes: Missing or non-numeric cells are excluded variable-by-variable. Correlation and regression use paired complete observations. Regression is ordinary least squares with one predictor. Results are educational and should be independently checked for formal research.
