ESG & Green Finance

Choose a topic to open its dedicated learning module with explanations, examples, classroom cases and BBA/MBA activities.

Environmental →

The environmental dimension of ESG considers how an organisation affects and depends on the natural environment, including climate, resources, pollution and biodiversity.

Social →

The social dimension of ESG examines how an organisation treats employees, customers, suppliers and communities, including safety, inclusion and human rights.

Governance →

Governance concerns how an organisation is directed, controlled and held accountable, including board oversight, ethics, transparency and shareholder rights.

Green Bonds →

Green bonds are debt instruments whose proceeds are intended to finance or refinance eligible green projects under a stated framework and disclosure approach.

Sustainable Finance →

Sustainable finance integrates relevant environmental, social and governance considerations into financing, investment and risk decisions.

ESG Reporting →

ESG reporting communicates an organisation's material sustainability-related risks, opportunities, impacts, policies, metrics and targets to stakeholders.
