Welcome, Finance Students! 👋

Financial Management is the process of planning, raising, investing and controlling financial resources to achieve the objectives of an organisation.

This page helps you learn Financial Management through simple explanations, formulas, examples and practice problems.

1️⃣ Financial Decisions

Financial management mainly involves three important decisions.

  • Investment Decision
  • Financing Decision
  • Dividend Decision
Example:
A company has ₹10 lakh. It must decide whether to invest in a new machine, borrow money or distribute profit to shareholders.

2️⃣ Capital Budgeting

Capital budgeting means evaluating long-term investment projects.

  • Payback Period
  • ARR
  • NPV
  • IRR
  • Profitability Index
NPV = Present Value of Cash Inflows − Initial Investment

3️⃣ Working Capital

Working capital is required for the day-to-day operations of a business.

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities
Example:
Current Assets = ₹5,00,000
Current Liabilities = ₹3,00,000

Working Capital = ₹2,00,000

4️⃣ Cost of Capital

Cost of capital is the minimum return expected by investors and lenders.

  • Cost of Debt
  • Cost of Preference Capital
  • Cost of Equity
  • WACC
WACC = Σ (Weight × Cost)

5️⃣ Leverage

Leverage shows the effect of fixed costs on business performance.

  • Operating Leverage
  • Financial Leverage
  • Combined Leverage
DOL = Contribution ÷ EBIT

6️⃣ Dividend Decision

Dividend decision determines how much profit should be distributed to shareholders and how much should be retained.

Example:
Profit = ₹10 lakh
Dividend paid = ₹4 lakh
Retained earnings = ₹6 lakh

7️⃣ Break-even Analysis

Break-even point is the level of sales where total revenue equals total cost.

BEP = Fixed Cost ÷ Contribution per Unit
Fixed Cost = ₹1,00,000
Selling Price = ₹100
Variable Cost = ₹60

Contribution = ₹40
BEP = 1,00,000 ÷ 40
BEP = 2,500 units

8️⃣ Financial Planning

Financial planning estimates the financial requirements of a business and determines how those requirements will be financed.

  • Estimate funds required
  • Determine sources of finance
  • Maintain liquidity
  • Control financial resources

9️⃣ Objectives of Financial Management

  • Profit Maximisation
  • Wealth Maximisation
  • Maintaining Liquidity
  • Efficient use of funds
  • Risk management
Key idea:
Modern financial management generally focuses on maximising shareholders' wealth while considering risk and return.

🧮 Practice Problem

A company has Current Assets of ₹8,00,000 and Current Liabilities of ₹5,00,000.

Question: Calculate Net Working Capital.

Solution:

Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities

= ₹8,00,000 − ₹5,00,000

Answer = ₹3,00,000

🎯 Think Like a Finance Manager

A company has ₹50 lakh available for investment. It can either purchase a new machine, repay debt, or distribute the money as dividend.

Question: What factors should the finance manager consider before making the decision?

  • Expected return
  • Risk
  • Cost of finance
  • Cash-flow requirements
  • Future growth opportunities
  • Shareholder expectations
