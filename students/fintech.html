🤖 FinTech

Understand how technology is changing payments, lending, investments, compliance and financial inclusion.

Back to Student Zone

Learning Notes

1. Meaning of FinTech

Financial technology uses digital tools and platforms to deliver or improve financial services. Examples include mobile payments, online lending, robo-advisory and digital insurance.

2. Digital payments in India

UPI enables instant bank-to-bank payments through supported apps and interfaces. Other payment methods include cards, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS, each with its own use cases and operating rules.

3. Digital lending

Digital lending uses technology for customer onboarding, data-based assessment, loan disbursal and servicing. Borrowers should verify the regulated lender, understand the Key Fact Statement and review all charges.

4. Artificial intelligence

AI can support fraud detection, customer service, credit assessment and investment research. Models require data quality, explainability, bias controls, privacy safeguards and human oversight.

5. Blockchain and distributed ledgers

A blockchain is a type of distributed ledger in which records are linked and maintained through a consensus process. Potential uses include settlement, traceability and tokenisation, but legal, security and scalability issues matter.

6. RegTech and SupTech

RegTech uses technology to help financial firms meet compliance obligations. SupTech uses technology by regulators and supervisors for oversight and analysis.

7. Cybersecurity and inclusion

Strong authentication, secure devices, careful handling of OTPs and prompt reporting of suspicious activity are essential. Digital finance can widen access, but connectivity, literacy and accessibility gaps remain.

Practice Questions with Solutions

1. Case study: Fraud prevention

A customer receives a call asking for an OTP to reverse a failed UPI transaction. What should the customer do?

Solution / Suggested answer: Do not share the OTP, PIN or password. End the call, contact the bank or payment provider through its official channel, and report suspicious activity promptly.

2. Calculation: Digital loan cost

A borrower receives ₹48,000 after ₹2,000 is deducted from a sanctioned loan of ₹50,000. What is the upfront deduction as a percentage of sanctioned amount?

Solution / Suggested answer: Upfront deduction percentage = 2,000 / 50,000 × 100 = 4%. The borrower should also examine interest, tenure and other charges to understand the full cost.

3. Theory question

Mention two risks of using AI in credit decisions.

Solution / Suggested answer: Risks include biased outcomes due to unrepresentative data and lack of explainability. Data privacy, model drift and errors also require controls.
