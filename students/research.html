4. Student research article workflow

1. Identify a business issue: Start from a company announcement, industry shift, innovation or policy change.
2. Frame the question: Make it specific, measurable and suitable for available data.
3. Review literature: Find relevant peer-reviewed papers and official reports; record author, year, method and findings.
4. Choose method: Select case study, survey, financial ratio analysis, content analysis or secondary-data analysis.
5. Analyse and report: Explain results, limitations, managerial implications and future research directions.

5. Suggested article structure

  1. Title and keywords
  2. Abstract and research objective
  3. Introduction and business context
  4. Literature review and research gap
  5. Research questions / hypotheses
  6. Methodology and data sources
  7. Analysis and findings
  8. Discussion and managerial implications
  9. Limitations and conclusion
  10. References using the required citation style
