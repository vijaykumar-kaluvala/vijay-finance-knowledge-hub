Suggested learning path for finance students

  1. Beginner: Start with a SWAYAM/NPTEL course or a foundation-level market learning module.
  2. Market-focused: Explore an appropriate NISM or NSE Academy module aligned with your career interest.
  3. Specialisation: Consider CFA for investment analysis, CMA for management/cost accounting, or ACCA for accountancy after checking eligibility, time and cost.
  4. Build evidence: Add practical projects such as company analysis, Excel financial models, mutual fund comparisons and research reports to your portfolio.

How to choose the right certification

Match the programme to your target role, not just its name. Compare the official syllabus, eligibility, total cost, exam format, time commitment, renewal requirements and employer relevance. Students should discuss long-term professional qualifications with an academic or career adviser before making a large financial commitment.
