📊 Financial Analysis Lab

Learn to read company accounts, calculate financial ratios, compare business performance and write evidence-based analyst reports.

← Back to Student Zone
Learning approach: Use actual annual reports and exchange filings for live company work. The worked example below is illustrative only; do not treat it as the financial performance of a real company.

1. Company financial statement analysis

Task A · Profitability

Read the income statement

Identify revenue, cost of goods sold, EBITDA, depreciation, finance cost, tax and profit after tax. Compare at least two financial years.

  • Revenue growth = (Current revenue − Previous revenue) / Previous revenue
  • Net profit margin = PAT / Revenue × 100
  • Operating margin = EBIT / Revenue × 100
Task B · Liquidity

Assess short-term strength

Use current assets and current liabilities to understand whether a company can meet near-term obligations.

  • Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities
  • Quick ratio = (Current assets − Inventory) / Current liabilities
  • Discuss the quality and ageing of receivables.
Task C · Solvency

Study capital structure

Review borrowings, equity, interest cost and cash flows. Explain whether debt appears manageable.

  • Debt-equity ratio = Total debt / Shareholders’ equity
  • Interest coverage = EBIT / Finance cost
  • Debt service capacity should also consider cash flow.

3. Student practical assignments

Assignment 1: Select one NSE/BSE listed company. Download its latest annual report and extract five-year revenue, EBIT, PAT, total assets, debt and equity.
Assignment 2: Compare two companies in the same industry using profitability, liquidity, efficiency and solvency ratios.
Assignment 3: Prepare a two-page analyst note with business model, financial trend, key risks, peer comparison and a reasoned conclusion.
