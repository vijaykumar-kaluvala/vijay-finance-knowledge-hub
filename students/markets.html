3. Weekly company analysis activity

Step 1 — Select: Choose one listed company from banking, IT, automobiles, pharmaceuticals, FMCG, energy or infrastructure.
Step 2 — Collect: Download the latest quarterly result, annual report and one exchange announcement. Record source URLs and retrieval date.
Step 3 — Analyse: Identify revenue and profit movement, debt, cash flow, key business segments, risks and management commentary.
Step 4 — Present: Prepare one chart, five bullet insights and one question for classroom discussion. Do not treat news headlines alone as investment advice.
