Learning Notes

1. Time value of money

Future Value: FV = PV(1 + r)^n. Present Value: PV = FV / (1 + r)^n. Here r is the period rate and n is the number of periods.

2. Cost of capital

After-tax cost of debt = Kd(1 − T), where Kd is pre-tax cost of debt and T is the tax rate, when interest is tax-deductible. Cost of equity may be estimated using CAPM: Ke = Rf + β(Rm − Rf).

3. Capital budgeting

NPV = Present value of cash inflows − Initial investment. Accept a conventional independent project when NPV is positive, subject to strategic and risk considerations. IRR is the discount rate that makes NPV zero.

4. Working capital

Net Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities. Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities. Interpret ratios with industry context and quality of assets.

5. Leverage

Degree of operating leverage measures EBIT sensitivity to sales. Degree of financial leverage measures EPS sensitivity to EBIT. Higher fixed commitments can increase both potential returns and risk.

6. Cost-volume-profit analysis

Contribution per unit = Selling Price − Variable Cost per Unit. Break-even units = Fixed Costs / Contribution per Unit.

7. Financial statement analysis

Use trend analysis, common-size statements and ratios together. Check accounting policies, one-off items, cash flows and industry conditions before drawing conclusions.

Practice Questions with Solutions

1. Problem 1: Break-even point

Selling price is ₹100 per unit, variable cost is ₹60 per unit and fixed costs are ₹80,000. Find break-even sales units.

Solution / Suggested answer: Contribution = ₹100 − ₹60 = ₹40. Break-even units = ₹80,000 / ₹40 = 2,000 units.

2. Problem 2: NPV

Initial investment is ₹10,000. Cash inflows are ₹6,000 at the end of each of the next two years. Discount rate is 10%. Find NPV.

Solution / Suggested answer: PV of inflows = 6,000/1.10 + 6,000/(1.10)^2 = ₹10,413.22 (approx.). NPV = ₹10,413.22 − ₹10,000 = ₹413.22. Accept on the NPV criterion.

3. Problem 3: WACC

Equity is 60% of capital with cost 15%; debt is 40% with pre-tax cost 10%; tax rate is 25%. Calculate WACC.

Solution / Suggested answer: After-tax debt cost = 10%(1 − 0.25) = 7.5%. WACC = (0.60 × 15%) + (0.40 × 7.5%) = 12%.

4. Problem 4: Current ratio

Current assets are ₹3,00,000 and current liabilities are ₹1,50,000.

Solution / Suggested answer: Current Ratio = 3,00,000 / 1,50,000 = 2:1.
