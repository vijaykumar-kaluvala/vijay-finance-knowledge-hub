📒 Accounting

Understand how businesses record transactions, prepare financial statements and use accounting information for decisions.

Back to Student Zone

Learning Notes

1. Accounting fundamentals

Accounting is the process of identifying, recording, classifying, summarising and communicating financial transactions. The main branches are financial accounting, cost accounting and management accounting.

2. Accounting equation

Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s Equity. Every transaction keeps this equation balanced. Example: The owner starts a business with ₹1,00,000 cash. Assets (cash) increase by ₹1,00,000 and capital increases by ₹1,00,000.

3. Journal and ledger

A journal records transactions chronologically using debit and credit. Ledger accounts group transactions by account. Example: Furniture purchased for cash ₹20,000: Debit Furniture A/c ₹20,000; Credit Cash A/c ₹20,000.

4. Trial balance

A trial balance lists ledger balances to check the arithmetical accuracy of postings. Equal debit and credit totals do not guarantee that every error has been identified.

5. Financial statements

The trading account determines gross profit, the profit and loss account determines net profit, and the balance sheet reports assets, liabilities and equity on a date.

6. Cost and management accounting

Cost accounting identifies and controls product or service costs. Management accounting uses budgets, variance analysis, marginal costing and other information for internal decisions.

7. Important ratios

Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities. Debt-Equity Ratio = Long-term Debt / Shareholders’ Funds. Net Profit Ratio = Net Profit / Revenue × 100.

Practice Questions with Solutions

1. Problem 1: Accounting equation

A business has assets of ₹5,00,000 and liabilities of ₹1,80,000. Find capital.

Solution / Suggested answer: Capital = Assets − Liabilities = ₹5,00,000 − ₹1,80,000 = ₹3,20,000.

2. Problem 2: Current ratio

Current assets are ₹2,40,000 and current liabilities are ₹1,20,000. Calculate current ratio.

Solution / Suggested answer: Current Ratio = 2,40,000 / 1,20,000 = 2:1.

3. Theory question

Differentiate financial accounting and management accounting.

Solution / Suggested answer: Financial accounting prepares general-purpose statements mainly for external users and follows applicable standards. Management accounting supports internal planning, control and decision-making; its reports are tailored to management needs.
