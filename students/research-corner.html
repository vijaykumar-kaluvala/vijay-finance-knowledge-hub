Learning Notes

1. Selecting a research topic

Choose a topic that is relevant, focused, researchable and supported by accessible data. Narrow broad themes into a population, period, location and measurable variables.

2. Possible finance research themes

Digital lending and financial inclusion; FinTech adoption and customer trust; ESG disclosure and firm performance; green finance and renewable investment; retail investor behaviour; market efficiency and volatility.

3. Research problem and objectives

A research problem identifies a gap or issue. Objectives should use clear action verbs such as examine, analyse, compare, assess or evaluate.

4. Literature review

Search scholarly databases and credible institutional sources. Record each study’s objective, sample, method, findings and limitations. Identify patterns and gaps rather than listing summaries only.

5. Research design and data

Specify whether the study is exploratory, descriptive or explanatory. Distinguish primary data from secondary data, define the sample and justify the sampling method and measurement scales.

6. Analysis and interpretation

Select methods that match the research question and data: descriptive statistics, correlation, regression, tests of differences or qualitative thematic analysis. Report assumptions, limitations and effect sizes where appropriate.

7. Research ethics and publication

Obtain informed consent where required, protect participant data, avoid plagiarism and fabrication, disclose conflicts and follow institutional ethics requirements. Verify journal scope, peer review, indexing claims and publication fees.

Practice Questions with Solutions

1. Research idea 1

Study the relationship between digital payment adoption and perceived transaction security among university students.

Solution / Suggested answer: Possible variables: frequency of digital payment use (dependent or outcome variable depending on model), perceived security, convenience, digital literacy and trust. Define the student population, sampling frame and questionnaire items.

2. Research idea 2

Examine ESG disclosure and financial performance in selected Indian listed companies.

Solution / Suggested answer: Possible measures: ESG disclosure score or a defined disclosure index, ROA, ROE and firm size. State the sample period, industry selection and data sources; association does not by itself prove causation.

3. Research checklist

What should a good research objective contain?

Solution / Suggested answer: It should be clear, specific, measurable or assessable, aligned with the research problem and feasible with available data and time.
