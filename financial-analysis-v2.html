Financial Analysis Lab

Enter a company's financial figures to calculate key profitability, liquidity, leverage and efficiency ratios. Review the interpretations and use them as a starting point for classroom discussion.

How to use this lab: Enter values in the input panel, then select Analyse figures. Use Load sample data to explore an example. Calculations run in this browser; your entries are not uploaded to a server.

1. Enter financial data

Use the same currency and scale for all amounts (for example, ₹ lakh). Use credible figures from the same reporting period.

If a required denominator is blank or zero, the related ratio is shown as unavailable.

2. Results & interpretation

Result cards update when you analyse the figures.

3. Ratio guide

Benchmarks vary by industry, business model, seasonality and accounting policy.

MeasureFormulaWhat it helps assess
Gross profit margin(Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100Profit retained after direct costs.
Net profit marginNet profit ÷ Revenue × 100Net earnings generated from sales.
Current ratioCurrent assets ÷ Current liabilitiesShort-term asset coverage of obligations.
Quick ratio(Current assets − Inventory) ÷ Current liabilitiesNear-term coverage excluding inventory.
Working capitalCurrent assets − Current liabilitiesShort-term funds available for operations.
Debt-to-equityTotal debt ÷ Shareholders' equityDebt relative to owners' funds.
ROANet profit ÷ Total assets × 100Profit relative to the asset base.
ROENet profit ÷ Shareholders' equity × 100Accounting return relative to equity.
Asset turnoverRevenue ÷ Total assetsSales generated per unit of assets.
Educational-use note: This tool provides mechanical calculations and general learning interpretations, not an audit, credit rating, investment recommendation or professional financial advice. Check the underlying statements, compare with industry peers and examine trends over multiple periods.
