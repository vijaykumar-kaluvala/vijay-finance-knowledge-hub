Finance Interview Practice

Open each question to review a suggested answer. Practise explaining the concept in your own words.

1. What is working capital?

Net working capital is current assets minus current liabilities. It helps assess a company's short-term financial position.

2. What is the difference between profit and cash flow?

Profit is calculated using accounting income and expenses. Cash flow tracks actual cash receipts and payments during a period.

3. What is the current ratio?

Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities. It is one measure of a company's short-term liquidity.

4. What is NPV?

Net Present Value is the present value of expected project cash inflows minus the present value of cash outflows, using a specified discount rate.

5. What is the difference between NPV and IRR?

NPV expresses a project's value in monetary terms at a specified discount rate. IRR is the discount rate at which the project's NPV equals zero.

6. What is diversification in investment?

Diversification spreads investments across different assets or exposures to reduce concentration risk. It does not eliminate all investment risk.

7. What is an NPA in banking?

NPA means Non-Performing Asset. It refers to a loan or advance classified as non-performing under the applicable regulatory rules.

8. Why is Excel useful in finance?

Excel supports calculations, budgeting, financial modelling, data organisation, reporting and analysis.

9. What is FinTech?

Financial technology uses digital tools and technology to deliver or improve financial products and services.

10. How would you analyse a company's financial performance?

Review its income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement. Calculate relevant ratios, compare trends across periods and consider industry context before drawing conclusions.
