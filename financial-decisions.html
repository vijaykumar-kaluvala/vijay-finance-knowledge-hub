Understanding Financial Decisions

Learn how businesses make investment, financing and dividend decisions to maximise value and achieve financial objectives.

1. Meaning of Financial Decisions

Financial decisions are decisions taken by a finance manager regarding how funds are raised, invested and distributed in a business.

Simple explanation: A business needs money to start operations, purchase assets, expand activities and reward shareholders. Financial decisions help determine where the money comes from and how it is used.

2. Objectives of Financial Decisions

  • Maximise shareholders' wealth.
  • Ensure optimum utilisation of financial resources.
  • Maintain adequate liquidity.
  • Balance risk and return.
  • Support long-term business growth.
  • Maintain an appropriate capital structure.

3. Three Major Financial Decisions

Investment Decision

Where should money be invested?

Choosing assets and projects such as machinery, buildings, technology and business expansion.

Example: Purchasing a new production machine.

Financing Decision

Where should money come from?

Selecting suitable sources of finance such as equity, debentures, bank loans and retained earnings.

Example: Raising a bank loan to expand operations.

Dividend Decision

How much profit should be distributed?

Deciding how much profit should be paid to shareholders and how much should be retained in the business.

Example: Paying dividends and retaining the remaining profit for expansion.

4. Practical Business Example

ABC Manufacturing Ltd. plans to expand its production capacity. The company requires ₹50 lakh for expansion.

DecisionCompany's action
Investment Purchase machinery worth ₹50 lakh.
Financing Raise ₹30 lakh through a bank loan and ₹20 lakh through equity.
Dividend Retain a portion of profits to support future expansion.
Learning point: All three decisions are interconnected. The investment creates a funding requirement, financing determines the source of funds, and dividend policy influences the availability of retained earnings.

5. Classroom Case Study

XYZ Ltd. has earned a profit of ₹20 lakh. It wants to purchase new machinery, repay part of its existing loan and distribute dividends to shareholders.

Discussion Questions

  1. Which decision is involved in purchasing new machinery?
  2. Which decision is involved in repaying or raising a loan?
  3. Which decision is involved in distributing dividends?
  4. Why should the company balance these three decisions?

Classroom activity: Divide students into groups. Ask each group to act as the finance team of XYZ Ltd. and recommend how the company should allocate its profit.

7. Key Takeaways

  • Investment decision: Where to invest funds.
  • Financing decision: How to raise funds.
  • Dividend decision: How to distribute or retain profits.
  • Effective financial decisions support business growth and shareholders' wealth maximisation.
