Financial Management

Financial Decisions →

How a business chooses where to invest, how to raise funds, how much profit to distribute and how to manage short-term funds.

Time Value of Money →

Money available today can be invested and may earn a return, so its value differs from the same nominal amount received later.

Capital Budgeting →

Capital budgeting evaluates long-term investments whose benefits and cash outflows extend over several years.

Cost of Capital →

Cost of capital is the required return expected by providers of finance and is used as a benchmark for investment decisions.

Capital Structure →

Capital structure is the long-term mix of debt and owners' funds used to finance a company's assets.

Working Capital →

Working capital management ensures that a business can meet short-term obligations while maintaining efficient investment in current assets.
