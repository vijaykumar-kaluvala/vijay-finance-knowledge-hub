Assignment 1 · Budgeting

Company Budget Preparation

Scenario: ABC Ltd. expects to sell 10,000 units next quarter at ₹500 per unit. Variable cost is ₹300 per unit and quarterly fixed costs are ₹8,00,000.

  • Prepare a sales budget and variable-cost budget.
  • Calculate the contribution and expected operating profit.
  • Explain two ways management could improve the budgeted result.
Show calculation guide

Sales = 10,000 × ₹500 = ₹50,00,000.

Variable cost = 10,000 × ₹300 = ₹30,00,000.

Contribution = ₹20,00,000.

Budgeted operating profit = ₹20,00,000 − ₹8,00,000 = ₹12,00,000.

Assignment 3 · Working Capital

Working Capital Management

Scenario: A company has current assets of ₹12 lakh and current liabilities of ₹8 lakh.

  • Calculate net working capital and the current ratio.
  • Explain the importance of maintaining liquidity.
  • Suggest ways to manage inventory, receivables and payables.
Show calculation guide

Net working capital = ₹12 lakh − ₹8 lakh = ₹4 lakh.

Current ratio = ₹12 lakh ÷ ₹8 lakh = 1.5:1.

Assignment 4 · Investment Decisions

Capital Budgeting Case Study

Scenario: A company is considering buying new equipment. The initial investment is ₹5,00,000. Expected annual cash inflows are ₹1,50,000 for four years.

  • Calculate the payback period, assuming equal annual inflows.
  • Explain why the time value of money matters.
  • Identify the additional information needed to calculate NPV and IRR.
Show calculation guide

Payback period = ₹5,00,000 ÷ ₹1,50,000 = approximately 3.33 years, assuming equal year-end cash inflows and no salvage value for this payback calculation.

NPV requires a specified discount rate. IRR is the rate at which NPV equals zero.
