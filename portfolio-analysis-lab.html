3. Classroom interpretation guide

  • Portfolio weight: holding amount ÷ total portfolio value × 100.
  • Weighted expected return: sum of (holding weight × expected return).
  • Expected annual gain: amount invested × expected return ÷ 100, summed across holdings.
  • Scenario value change: sum of (amount invested × scenario change ÷ 100).

Educational-use note: these are mechanical calculations based on user-entered assumptions, not investment advice, a return guarantee or a complete measure of portfolio risk. Real outcomes depend on market movements, correlations, costs, taxes, liquidity and investor circumstances.
