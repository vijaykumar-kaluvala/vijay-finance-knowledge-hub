Banking & Financial Institutions

Choose a topic to open its dedicated learning module with explanations, examples, classroom cases and BBA/MBA activities.

Commercial Banks →

Commercial banks accept deposits, provide loans and payment services, and perform financial intermediation within the regulatory framework.

Central Banking →

A central bank supports monetary and financial stability and performs functions such as monetary policy, currency issuance and banking-system oversight according to its mandate.

Credit & Loans →

Credit allows a borrower to use funds now and repay according to agreed terms. Loan assessment considers ability and willingness to repay.

Credit Rating →

A credit rating expresses an agency's opinion about the relative credit risk of an issuer or debt instrument; it is not a guarantee of repayment.

NPA Concepts →

A non-performing asset is a loan or advance classified as non-performing under applicable regulatory norms when repayment remains overdue beyond prescribed criteria, subject to product-specific rules.

Financial Markets →

Financial markets bring together participants who issue, buy and sell financial claims, helping mobilise savings, allocate capital and manage risk.
