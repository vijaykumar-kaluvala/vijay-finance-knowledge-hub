Investment & Stock Market

Choose a topic to open its dedicated learning module with explanations, examples, classroom cases and BBA/MBA activities.

Equity Shares →

An equity share represents ownership in a company and may provide voting rights and a residual claim on profits and assets.

IPO →

An initial public offering is a company's first offer of shares to public investors, subject to applicable regulatory and exchange requirements.

Bonds →

A bond is a debt instrument under which an issuer promises specified payments according to its terms, including principal repayment where applicable.

Mutual Funds →

A mutual fund pools money from investors and invests according to a stated scheme objective and portfolio mandate.

Risk & Return →

Investment return measures the reward earned, while risk reflects uncertainty around outcomes and the possibility of loss.

Portfolio Basics →

A portfolio is a collection of investments held by an investor. Portfolio construction considers objectives, risk tolerance, time horizon and diversification.
