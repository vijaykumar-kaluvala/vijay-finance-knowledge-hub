3. How to verify a journal before submission

  1. Find the exact journal title and ISSN on its official publisher website.
  2. Search the title or ISSN in Scopus Sources.
  3. Check whether the journal is active or discontinued and inspect its coverage years.
  4. Search the same title in the Clarivate Master Journal List.
  5. Confirm the exact Web of Science collection, if indexed.
  6. Check the publisher's submission guidelines, publication fees and peer-review policy.
  7. Save the date of verification for your research records.
Researcher alert: Never rely only on a journal's claim of being Scopus indexed, Web of Science indexed, UGC approved or Q1. Verify the exact title and ISSN in the official source database. Quartiles and indexing coverage should be recorded for the relevant year and subject category.
