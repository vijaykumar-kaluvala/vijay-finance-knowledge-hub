Financial Markets

Financial markets bring together participants who issue, buy and sell financial claims, helping mobilise savings, allocate capital and manage risk.

1. Concept and meaning

Financial markets bring together participants who issue, buy and sell financial claims, helping mobilise savings, allocate capital and manage risk.

Core idea: Primary issuance raises funds for an issuer; secondary trading generally transfers ownership between investors and supports liquidity and price discovery.

2. Key concepts and tools

Market classification includes money market and capital market, and primary and secondary markets. These classify markets by maturity or transaction stage.

Use the relevant formula or analytical framework only when its assumptions fit the problem. State units, time period and assumptions clearly.

3. Worked example / practical illustration

A company issuing new shares uses the primary market; an investor later selling those shares on an exchange participates in the secondary market.

4. Classroom case study

Students map how a company, investor, broker, exchange, depository and regulator interact in a securities transaction.

Discussion prompts

  • What information is missing before making a recommendation?
  • What are the financial and non-financial risks?
  • Which assumptions should be tested?

5. Learning activity

BBA: BBA: classify instruments and market segments.

MBA: MBA: analyse market structure, liquidity, price discovery and regulation.
