Management Accounting

Management accounting provides financial and non-financial information to managers for planning, control and decision-making.

1. Concept and meaning

Management accounting provides financial and non-financial information to managers for planning, control and decision-making.

Core idea: Management accounting is internally oriented and adapts information to the decision; it complements rather than replaces financial accounting.

2. Key concepts and tools

Contribution = Sales − Variable Cost. Break-even units = Fixed Cost / Contribution per Unit. Budget variance = Actual − Budget, with favourable/unfavourable interpretation depending on the item.

Use the relevant formula or analytical framework only when its assumptions fit the problem. State units, time period and assumptions clearly.

3. Worked example / practical illustration

Selling price ₹100, variable cost ₹60 and fixed cost ₹20,000 give contribution ₹40 per unit and break-even volume 500 units.

4. Classroom case study

A company considers a price discount to increase sales. Students estimate contribution, capacity use and the additional volume needed to maintain profit.

Discussion prompts

  • What information is missing before making a recommendation?
  • What are the financial and non-financial risks?
  • Which assumptions should be tested?

5. Learning activity

BBA: BBA: prepare budgets and basic variance analysis.

MBA: MBA: apply CVP, relevant costing, performance measures and strategic decision support.

7. Key takeaway

Management accounting is internally oriented and adapts information to the decision; it complements rather than replaces financial accounting.
