NPA Concepts

A non-performing asset is a loan or advance classified as non-performing under applicable regulatory norms when repayment remains overdue beyond prescribed criteria, subject to product-specific rules.

1. Concept and meaning

A non-performing asset is a loan or advance classified as non-performing under applicable regulatory norms when repayment remains overdue beyond prescribed criteria, subject to product-specific rules.

Core idea: NPA classification, provisioning and recovery are governed by current RBI directions; use the applicable rules for the relevant loan type and date.

2. Key concepts and tools

Gross NPA ratio = Gross NPAs / Gross Advances × 100%. Net NPA considers provisions and other regulatory adjustments as defined by the applicable framework.

Use the relevant formula or analytical framework only when its assumptions fit the problem. State units, time period and assumptions clearly.

3. Worked example / practical illustration

If gross NPAs are ₹5 crore and gross advances are ₹100 crore, the gross NPA ratio is 5%.

4. Classroom case study

A bank reports rising overdue loans in its SME portfolio. Students examine early-warning signals, recovery options, provisioning and effects on profitability.

BBA: BBA: understand NPA terminology and calculate ratios.

MBA: MBA: analyse asset quality, provisioning, recovery strategy and credit concentration.
