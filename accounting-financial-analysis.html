Accounting & Financial Analysis

Choose a topic to open its dedicated learning module with explanations, examples, classroom cases and BBA/MBA activities.

Financial Statements →

Financial statements summarise an entity's financial position, performance and cash flows for users making economic decisions.

Ratio Analysis →

Ratio analysis converts financial-statement figures into relationships that help evaluate liquidity, profitability, efficiency and solvency.

Cost Accounting →

Cost accounting identifies, classifies, records and analyses costs to support costing, control and operational decisions.

Management Accounting →

Management accounting provides financial and non-financial information to managers for planning, control and decision-making.

Cash Flow →

Cash-flow analysis examines cash generated and used in operating, investing and financing activities.

Budgeting →

Budgeting translates organisational plans into quantified financial and operational targets for a defined period.
