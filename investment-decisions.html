2. Risk and Expected Return (CAPM)

CAPM estimates expected return using the risk-free rate, beta and expected market return. Enter rates as percentages.

Market risk premium
—
Market return minus risk-free rate.
CAPM expected return
—
Risk-free rate + beta × market risk premium.

6. Investment Decision Checklist

Before making a decision, students should consider:

  • What return is expected, and over what time period?
  • What market, business, inflation and liquidity risks could affect the investment?
  • Are the valuation assumptions reasonable and supported by evidence?
  • Is the portfolio diversified, and are the weights consistent with the investor's goals?
  • How sensitive is the result to changes in interest rates, growth assumptions or selling price?
